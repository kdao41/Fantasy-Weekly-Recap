The Lmphm Heist: Grand Larceny in Plain Sight

Let's start with the crime scene. lmphm is 8-1, sitting atop the standings like a man who just found a wallet and is hoping nobody asks questions. The all-play record says 63-36, which is good. Genuinely good. It is also fifth best in this league. The scoreboard says "league champion." The math says "decent guy who got a few favors from the schedule gods." That's 2.3 wins the universe handed him for free, and the receipts are everywhere.

Exhibit A: in Week 8, DVTV scored 140.3 points and lost to lmphm. One hundred and forty points. In most weeks that's a win, a trophy, and a smug group chat message. DVTV got a loss and a lesson in how cruel this hobby can be. lmphm didn't beat DVTV, he mugged him. Meanwhile, his season high of 187.7 in Week 5 is a real, legitimate monster, and I'll give him that. I'll also note that nobody has seen that guy since. Josh Allen's 40.8 in Week 2 was lovely too. A man can be good and lucky at once. That's the problem. Only one of those is sustainable.

Next up is anth0nyng, who's 6-3 with an all-play record of 45-54. Read that again. He has a winning record and a losing all-play, which means that against the whole league he's been below average and somehow third in the standings. He has scored 1028.8 points, which is fewer than wabaki's 1202.9 by a gap you could park a boat in. His closest win was 116-114.8 over PutItOnYa, a game decided by 1.2 points, so he's literally one wobbly kicker away from 5-4. And this week he plays lmphm. Two frauds, one matchup. Somebody's lie is getting exposed, and honestly I'm just here with popcorn.

Free wabaki: The Most Robbed Man in America

Now the tragedy. wabaki is 4-5. He leads the entire league in points with 1202.9. He leads in all-play at 70-29, the best true record in the league by a mile. He's 2.4 wins unlucky, the worst luck in the league, and he sits seventh in the standings. Seventh! He's the best team in the league and he's currently outside the playoff line looking in through the window like a kid watching a birthday party he wasn't invited to.

He dropped 148 this week, the Week 9 high, because of course he did. He once beat keveezy 161.6 to 76.7 in Week 4, the biggest blowout of the year, and the man still can't buy a win when it matters. Somebody in this league owes wabaki an apology and probably a fruit basket. If you want a villain, look at the schedule. It has personally wronged this man.

And here's the dare. Week 10 he plays jimbo817, who's 5-4 with an all-play of 64-35, a perfectly respectable team that is also exactly what wabaki should be stepping on. If wabaki loses this one, I'm calling for a formal investigation. Jimbo, I mean this with love: Jahmyr Gibbs' 41.4 in Week 3 is the best thing you've done all year and it's been six weeks. Prove me wrong.

Odeh has one great receiver and a lot of vibes.

DVTV sits fourth at 5-4 with a 65-34 all-play, second best in the league. He's the Week 8 victim and the second most robbed team in the league behind wabaki. If you're scoring 140 and losing, you have two choices: cry or get angry. I recommend angry. He gets oooEcho in Week 10, a team that's 5-4 with a 48-51 all-play and CeeDee Lamb's 41.3 as its main selling point. Take your frustration out on them, DVTV. They have it coming.

And donblood jumped two spots in the movers column, which is nice, except he's 3-6 and has a 1167.8 points against, the worst in the league. He's been fed a steady diet of opponents having their best week against him. His reward is a Week 10 date with kholabear, which is basically a bye with a handshake.

The Basement: Where Hope Goes to Die

Let's pay respects to the cellar. kholabear is 1-8 with 861.3 points, the fewest in the league, and an all-play record of 23-76. That is not a bad team. That's a crime scene. keveezy is 2-7 with a 25-74 all-play and just posted the week's low of 74.6, a number I'd be embarrassed to score in a pickup game of Madden. These two have combined for three wins. Three! Some of you have had single weeks with that many bench guys worth starting.

The nice part is that Week 10 brings us the Basement Bowl in spirit, if not in name: keveezy takes on PutItOnYa, and kholabear faces donblood. Each of them has a legitimate shot at a win, which for these two is the equivalent of a lottery ticket. I'm predicting at least one of them cashes in, probably keveezy against PutItOnYa, whose 4-5 record is built on a 42-57 all-play and a general lack of conviction.

Final prediction: wabaki finally gets his win over jimbo817, lmphm's perfect cover gets blown by anth0nyng, and the standings finally start to look like the points. And if I'm wrong, I'll personally eat the column. Screenshot it. I dare you.
